Lvls! · share what you gathered

While you play, Lvls! notes where you meet npcs and objects, who starts and ends quests and what counts for objectives. Shared, it fixes the guide for everyone on WoW Forever.

  1. In the game, type /lvls share.
  2. Press Ctrl+C in the box that opens (the text is already selected).
  3. Paste it below and press Share.

A share holds no character, realm or account names: a random id for your install, the game data above and the Lvls! version. Sharing again replaces your earlier share.
